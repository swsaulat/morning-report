Privacy notice

Morning Report Attendance System · Adult Emergency Department, Security Forces Hospital – Riyadh

What we collect

  • From your Google sign-in: your name and email address only, to confirm your identity.
  • What you enter: full name, Staff/Badge ID and training level.
  • At the moment you register: date and time, your phone's location reading (to confirm you are in the classroom) and a short device description (for example "iPhone · Safari").

Why

Solely to record attendance at the departmental Morning Report, to prevent duplicate or proxy registrations, and to send the daily attendance report to the Head of Adult Emergency, the ED & SBEM secretary and the SBEM Training Program Director.

What we do not do

  • We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • We do not track your location outside the single registration moment.
  • We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Records are kept in a Google Sheet owned by the system administrator, with read-only access for the designated department recipients. Daily report PDFs are kept in the same Google Drive.

Questions or corrections

Contact the chief resident or the system administrator (swsaulat@gmail.com) to correct or remove a record.
